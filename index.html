About the Academy

Learn the skill. Discover your style.

Sri Maruthi Kalaikoodam is a creative learning space focused on drawing, painting, sketching and practical art development.

Our approach combines structured practice, guided learning and creative exploration so learners can develop stronger observation, hand control, colour understanding and artistic confidence.

Every artwork begins with a simple line — our goal is to help you turn that line into an idea.
